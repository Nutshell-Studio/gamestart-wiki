    Talents & Gear

    Talents are live end to end: earn talent points, spend them in the pause menu's Skills tab, and cast skills with keys 1–6.

    Talent points

    One currency: talent points — money is gone from the store entirely. You earn them two ways:

    • Quizzes (the fast lane): complete the Wall of Shame correctly and get paid in talents. The Hall of Honor is planned.
    • Sorting (the slow trickle): regular tidying XP slowly converts into talent points on its own.

    The Skills tab

    Open the pause menu and pick Skills to spend talents:

    • Your unspent talent points show at the top.
    • Available Active Skills appear first, in skill-bar order, followed by Passives. Active entries wear their current key badge; Passives have no key badge.
    • Levels get pricier as you climb: level 1 costs 3 talent points, level 2 5, level 3 7, and so on (two more each level). The Upgrade button always shows the exact price. Each entry shows your level (like Lv 2/10), the cooldown, and what every level improves; press Upgrade to buy the next one. A maxed skill says MAX.

    The skill bar (keys 1–6)

    The skill bar always keeps six positions. Press keyboard keys 1–6 to cast. (The skill keys are keyboard-only for now — no gamepad binding yet.)

    • Unused positions say empty, unavailable skills say locked, and learned skills show their level.
    • A cast only "spends" the skill when it actually does something. If it can't work right now, a short hint pops up instead — like "Hold a box first", "On cooldown", or "No charges left" — and no cooldown starts.
    • Cooldowns refill on their own; higher levels mostly shorten them.

    Active skills

    Key Skill What it does
    1 Shelf Guide Highlights the shelf where the box in your hands belongs — a beacon marks the spot for a short time; higher levels cut the cooldown
    2 Tendril Grab Reels copies of the game you're holding in from across your district — shelf fronts and loose strays alike — straight into your hands, up to eight a cast. Level 2 reaches the districts next to you as well; level 3 digs behind the front copy; level 4 pulls from anywhere in the store and brings the whole catch in at once instead of one at a time
    3 Pip-Boy Opens a large INFONET readout for the box you're holding. You can keep moving and handling boxes, but other skills wait; press 3 or Esc to close it. Dropping or throwing the scanned box also closes it
    4 Portal Return Set a return pad at your feet, then aim at a shelf slot to link it — boxes you set down on the pad zip straight to that shelf
    5 Bubble Carry Seals the box in your hands inside a bubble that floats it to its home shelf on its own — your hands stay free. If the shelf is full when it arrives, the bubble pops and the box settles nearby
    6 Portal Pistol Arm it, then Left Click a wall or the floor to open the orange portal and Left Click again for the blue one — step into either and you walk out of the other, keeping your speed. Right Click cancels while you're aiming; the pair fades on its own after about half a minute

    Passive skills

    Passives work all the time once learned — no key, no cooldown:

    Skill What it does
    Deluxe Cart Carry more boxes at once — two levels, each raising your carry limit further
    Plumbob Guide While you hold a box, a hovering gem marks the shelf where it belongs
    Stock Sense While you hold a box, nearby copies of the same game get a purple outline — the "reunite the stack" glow

    A note on the glow: highlighting matching games used to be free for everyone. It now belongs to Stock Sense — if you liked it, it's a cheap early buy.

    The retired gadget shelf

    Gadgets were physical store gear — each a loving parody of infamous game-industry hardware. The classic shelf has since retired, its powers grown up and shipped as proper skills: the Sorter's Magnet's grab lives on in Tendril Grab, the Spotter Visor's scanner vision ships as the Pip-Boy skill and the Stock Sense glow, the Deluxe Pack ships as the Deluxe Cart passive, and the Pocket Terminal's database stays where it always lived — the counter terminal. The Buddy clocked out with the rest of the classic shelf. New gadgets may arrive with later store phases.

    A note for quiz fans: quiz fixtures are designed to be solvable bare-handed — skills make them comfortable, never required.
