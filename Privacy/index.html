    Privacy — the optional Steam connection

    Planned for Steam-enabled builds. The secure connection remains unavailable in the current pre-release until its Steam setup and on-Steam checks are complete.

    When available, GameStart can put your own Steam library on the store's shelves. This page is the plain-English record of exactly what that means. The short version: it is opt-in and read-only. Limited library details pass through GameStart's online library service and may be held there for up to six hours.

    The service runs on Cloudflare's network. Processing and temporary storage may happen in places where Cloudflare operates its service. Before this connection is made available, this page will name the country or countries used for temporary storage.

    The connection starts off. You can enable it from the catalog screen; only your saved choice can turn it on. Installing or enabling a pack cannot connect Steam for you.

    What happens if you opt in

    • The game asks the Steam app for a short-lived proof that you are signed in. It never asks for your Steam password.
    • GameStart's library service checks that proof with Steam, then requests your owned game titles, playtime totals, and last-played times. The service may temporarily keep your Steam account ID and your full owned-games list for no longer than six hours.
    • To prevent automated abuse, the service also keeps your connection address in a daily request counter for about 25 hours.
    • The game downloads that limited list and fetches cover art onto your machine.
    • That data is used to fill the shelves, print the boxes, and (in the planned quiz fixtures) generate quizzes about your play history.
    • Cover art is cached locally so later sessions load faster.
    • If you host a co-op session, the resulting shelf entries can be shown to the players who join you. Your sign-in proof and Steam password are not shared with them.

    What never happens

    • Nothing from your library is bundled into the game — every player's store is prepared from the account that opted in.
    • The game cannot buy, sell, launch, or modify anything on your Steam account.
    • GameStart's publishing key is kept on the online service; it is not included in the game.

    If you opt out (or your profile is private)

    You get the fictional catalog — a full store of original parody games with authored stats. Everything works, including the planned quizzes (they just quiz the fictional catalog instead of your backlog). You can change your mind either way at any time; the store restocks accordingly. Turning the connection off prevents new library requests. The service copy expires within six hours; locally cached cover art and the last downloaded list can remain until you clear the game's local data.
